title=Sliding Gates
description=Animated sliding chain-link and wooden gates for Build 42 in 2-, 4- and 6-cell widths.
description=
description=Features:
description=- Chain-link (Welding) and wooden (Carpentry) gates, 2, 4 or 6 cells wide.
description=- Build quality depends on your skill: low, medium or high, each with its own look. Built gates keep their quality.
description=- Maximum gate health is your building skill level x 300. Damage is shown visually in three stages.
description=- Build gates in eight placements (press R while placing to rotate, reverse or flip to the outer side).
description=- Smooth sliding animation with cell-by-cell collision; vehicles in the leaf's path block movement.
description=- Recover an electric motor from washing machines and dryers, craft a gate drive and mount it on an existing gate.
description=- Power the drive from the grid or a car battery; grid power recharges the battery.
description=- Craft an RF remote, pair it with a drive and operate the gate from up to 20 tiles (F9 by default, rebindable). Fits on a key ring.
description=- Whole-assembly dismantling with material recovery.
description=- Translated into all vanilla languages.
description=
description=Multiplayer support.
description=
description=HOW TO USE
description=
description=1. Build the gate
description=- Chain-link: Building menu → Welding → Sliding Gate (2, 4 or 6 cells).
description=- Wooden: Building menu → Carpentry → Wooden Sliding Gate (2, 4 or 6 cells). Needs a hammer and a saw.
description=- 6-cell gates open in 9 seconds (2-cell: 3 s, 4-cell: 6 s) and need a 6-cell free pocket for the leaf.
description=- Minimum skill is 4. Quality: Welding 4–5 low, 6–7 medium, 8+ high; Carpentry 4 low, 5–6 medium, 7+ high. The menu shows the tier that matches your current skill.
description=- While placing, press R repeatedly. It cycles through EIGHT placements: north edge, west edge, each also reversed, and each of those on the outer (south/east) side of the same edge. Reversed gates slide the other way, with the motor post at the opposite end. Outer gates use the same cells but put the leaf, rails and drive on the other side of the fence line.
description=- Check the ghost preview, then place the gate.
description=
description=2. Install the drive
description=- Disassemble washing machines or dryers for a 30% chance to recover an electric motor.
description=- Crafting menu → Electrical → Assemble Sliding Gate Drive (Electrical 3, Welding 2).
description=- The recipe requires the motor, metal parts, electronics scrap, electrical wire, wire, a radio receiver and fasteners. You also need an unbroken screwdriver and wrench, a welding mask, a blowtorch with fuel and welding rods.
description=- To install the finished drive you need Electrical 1, Mechanics 1, an unbroken screwdriver and wrench, the drive, and 4 fasteners (screws, nuts and bolts, or a mixture).
description=- Stand next to the gate's motor post, right-click → Install Gate Drive.
description=
description=3. Power it
description=- With grid power, the drive works right away.
description=- With no grid power, put ONE car battery into the drive: right-click the gate → Put battery into drive. Your character walks over and inserts it.
description=- Right-click → Take battery from drive to retrieve it. The "Drive battery compartment" in the loot window next to the post works too.
description=- Grid power recharges the battery.
description=- Right-click the gate → Open using drive / Close using drive.
description=- Empty the battery compartment before removing the drive.
description=
description=4. Pair the remote
description=- Crafting menu → Electrical → Assemble Gate Remote (Electrical 2; needs an unbroken screwdriver).
description=- Keep the remote in your inventory, stand near the gate with the drive, right-click → Link gate remote.
description=- Use the remote from your inventory or press F9 (rebind it in Options → Key bindings).
description=- Range is 20 tiles from the drive on the same floor, and walls don't block it. One remote controls one drive.
description=
description=Recipes:
description=- Chain-link gates: Building menu → Welding.
description=- Wooden gates: Building menu → Carpentry.
description=- Gate drive and RF remote: Crafting menu → Electrical.
description=
description=Debug: with Build Cheat enabled, any gate variant can be placed regardless of skill.
